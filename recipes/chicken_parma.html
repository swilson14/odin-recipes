Chicken Parma

Delicious Chicken Parma!

Ingredients

  • Chicken schnitzel
  • Ham
  • Cheese
  • Napoli sauce

Steps

  1. Air fry chicken schnitzel for 12 minutes at 180 degrees, turning half way.
  2. Put chicken schnitzel on baking tray.
  3. Put a slice of ham on top of the schnitzel.
  4. Pour napoli sauce over the schnitzel.
  5. Put a slice of cheese on top.
  6. Bake in oven for 10 minutes or until cheese is melted.
  7. Enjoy your delicious chicken parma!
