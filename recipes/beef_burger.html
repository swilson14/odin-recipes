Beef Burger

Delicious Beef Burger!

Ingredients

  • Beef burger
  • Bread roll
  • Cheese
  • BBQ Sauce
  • Rocket lettuce

Steps

  1. Pan fry beef burger until cooked through, rotating every few minutes.
  2. Cut buns in half.
  3. Put burger on one half of the bun.
  4. Add BBQ sauce on top of burger.
  5. Add slice of cheese on top of burger.
  6. Add generous serving of rocket on top of cheese.
  7. Add second half of bun on top.
  8. Enjoy your delicious burger!
