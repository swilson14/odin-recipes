Delicious Tacos

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Tortillas
  • Shredded Cheese
  • Taco seasoning
  • Taco sauce
  • Lettuce

Steps

  1. Cook ground beef in frying pan until brown and cooked through.
  2. Add taco seasoning and one cup of water to pan.
  3. Stir while cooking to mix seasoning and reduce water.
  4. Add mince to tortillas.
  5. Add a tablespoon of taco sauce.
  6. Add lettuce and sprinkle shredded cheese on top.
  7. Enjoy your delcious tacos!
